What OCSDN Does

From local hustle to structured opportunity.

OCSDN is intended to provide a structured environment where small businesses can register, identify their needs, connect with suppliers and participate in organised commercial opportunities.

🍔

Township Food Businesses

Kota shops, takeaways, fast-food businesses and other local food entrepreneurs can be part of a structured customer network.

🏪

Local Commerce

OCSDN is designed around the reality of township commerce: local customers, local entrepreneurs, local suppliers and practical business opportunities.

🤝

Supplier Connections

The network can help participating businesses identify suppliers and organise procurement requirements rather than operating completely alone.

How OCSDN Works

A simple business connection model.

The platform is being designed around a straightforward flow from registration to commercial activity.

1

Register

Businesses and suppliers provide the information required to participate in the network.

2

Identify Needs

A customer can record procurement, product or business requirements.

3

Connect

OCSDN can facilitate structured connections between customers and participating suppliers.

4

Track

Orders, fees, commercial records and activity can be tracked through the digital platform.
